A Nedlands garden should look like it belongs to the house.

Nedlands streets mix established character homes with big new builds, often on the same block of the street. The garden is what ties the house to its street, so we design it around the architecture, the trees you want to keep and how your family uses the space.

Greg walks every block himself. He reads the levels, the drainage and where the afternoon sun lands, then draws a design you sign off before a single order goes in. The crew who builds it is the crew he briefs, so what you approve is what goes in the ground.

Nedlands questions

Straight answers before you call.

Do you design and build, or just design?

Both. The person who designs your Nedlands garden briefs the crew who builds it, so what is drawn is what goes in the ground.

How much does landscape design cost in Nedlands?

A full landscape design is $990 + GST, non-refundable. The build itself depends on the block, the scope and the materials, and Greg gives you an honest number after the site visit, before you commit to anything.

Can you work with my architect or builder?

Yes. We regularly work alongside architects, builders and designers, and can pick up from their plans or design the landscape from scratch.

Do you handle front gardens and verges?

Yes. Front gardens, verges and streetscapes are part of most Nedlands projects. Where the City of Nedlands needs an application for verge works, we prepare it with you.

How soon can you start?

We take on a limited number of projects at a time and usually book a few months ahead. Get in touch early, especially for spring and summer builds.
